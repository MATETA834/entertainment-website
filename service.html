1. Music and Singing

Music is a powerful way to express emotions, tell stories, and connect with people. HTM Entertainment welcomes interest from singers, musicians, vocal performers, and music artists who want to develop and share their talent.

We aim to create opportunities for musical creativity and encourage connections between performers and entertainment projects. Whether you are an emerging singer or an established performer, you can contact us to discuss your interests and ambitions.

Areas you can discuss with us

  • Introducing your singing or music talent.
  • Discussing music projects and collaborations.
  • Exploring performance opportunities.
  • Sharing information about your songs and work.
  • Discussing ways to present your talent to audiences.
Who is this for? Singers, vocalists, musicians, songwriters, and other music creatives.
🎬

2. Acting, Filmmaking and Movies

Films and acting bring ideas, characters, and stories to life. HTM Entertainment welcomes actors, actresses, filmmakers, scriptwriters, and other film creatives who are interested in the entertainment industry.

We aim to encourage storytelling and collaboration among people who want to participate in film-related projects. Creative projects can be discussed according to their requirements, available opportunities, and the skills of the people involved.

Areas you can discuss with us

  • Introducing yourself as an actor or actress.
  • Discussing film and movie project ideas.
  • Exploring possible casting or acting opportunities.
  • Connecting with other film creatives.
  • Sharing acting portfolios, showreels, or previous work.
Who is this for? Actors, actresses, filmmakers, scriptwriters, directors, and aspiring film performers.

Having talent is important, but introducing that talent to the right audience can also make a difference. HTM Entertainment aims to help talented people discuss ways of presenting their abilities, creative work, and achievements.
