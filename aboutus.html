Who We Are

A Home for Talent and Creativity

HTM Entertainment is an entertainment company based in Kigali City, Rwanda. We are dedicated to promoting talent, creativity, and entertainment by bringing together talented people from different industries.

Our areas of interest include music and singing, acting, filmmaking, comedy, artistic performance, and sports. We believe that talented individuals deserve opportunities to showcase their abilities, connect with audiences, and develop their potential.

We aim to encourage collaboration, celebrate originality, and help create opportunities for entertainers and sports players to gain recognition and grow in their respective fields.
